" Oura Identity Digital Brand Agency"

Beyond Design, We Build Identities.

I built this website for my digital brand agency, Oura Identity, to bring together all our digital marketing services and a live AI automation tool in one place. Instead of creating a simple static page, I wanted to build something practical that visitors and clients can actually interact with.

Live Website: [https://ruqayyashafi17-source.github.io/Oura-Identity-AI-Growth-Hub/]

" What This Project Includes"

- Home Page (index.html): Introduces Oura Identity, our core capabilities, an interactive stack switcher, and a quick goal finder that recommends the right marketing or automation solution.
- Services & Scope Builder (services.html): Lists all 10 agency services including Brand Identity & Logo Design, Social Media Management, Posts & Videos, Copywriting, Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO & GEO, Google Business Profile setup, Website Design, and n8n AI Workflows. Visitors can filter services, build a custom package brief, or send any service directly to the AI generator.
- Live AI Post & Hashtag Generator (ai-generator.html): Connected to my live n8n webhook workflow. Users can pick a campaign preset or enter their own brand, topic, platform, and goal to generate ready-to-use post copy and hashtags in real time.
- Brand Audit & Portfolio (ai-audit.html & portfolio.html): Showcases agency work and digital growth diagnostics.

" Tools & Technologies Used"

- HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript
- n8n Workflow Automation & Webhooks
